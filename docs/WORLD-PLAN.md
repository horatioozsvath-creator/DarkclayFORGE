# The living Reach: a plan (milestones 19–24)

Status: planned and decided (2026-11-16), nothing built. Builds on release 37.

## What it's for

Right now the Reach is kind: nothing happens to the shop that the player didn't choose. The late game also piles up coin with little to
spend it on (BALANCE-NOTES: 5,000–13,000 c a day by week four). This plan adds a world that acts on its own: thieves, monsters, a forge
that wears out, a bank, and a runner who can come to harm. Each threat has a warning, a defence you can buy or make, and a way back.

## Rules for every threat

1. **Seen coming.** Every threat shows first: rumours on the notice board, a sign on the door, the forge's wear. Nothing lands without
   a warning the player could act on.
2. **A defence for coin, and a better one you make.** Guards and locks can be bought; the best wards, armour and weapons for the Watch
   are made at your own stations. The crafting stays the heart of the game.
3. **Never ruinous.** A bad night costs a share of what's on show, never the save. Coin in the bank is always safe. Relics and Mythics
   in the Hall are never taken. Every loss can be won back or rebuilt.
4. **Scaled to the shop.** Threats grow with renown and what's on the shelves: a new shop isn't worth robbing, a famed one is.
5. **A gentler Reach.** Each save can turn the threats off (a switch on the title screen's new game, and under the Bank).
6. **Told plainly.** What happened while you were away is the first thing you see on return, with what it cost and what to do next.

## The systems

### The Bank (a new tab beside STAFF)
- **Deposits:** coin in the bank can't be stolen. A small interest each week (1 %), capped so it's never a money machine.
- **Loans:** from KNOWN, borrow up to a limit set by renown (KNOWN 1,000 c up to OF THE REACH 40,000 c). **Interest is 5 % a week**,
  taken every seven days from your coin (or the bank, if you choose). Repay any time, all or part.
- **Missing a payment:** the first time, a letter and a late fee. The second, **the bailiff** comes in person and takes pieces from the
  shelf to the value owed (never Relics or Mythics; the Hall is never touched), and renown drops a little. The debt never grows past
  twice what was borrowed.
- **While away:** interest is **paused**: it's only taken for weeks in which the game was opened. A long break costs nothing.
- **What it's for:** buying the second shop, the Hall or a station early, then paying it back from what it earns.

### Wear, and the forge that breaks
- **Every station wears** with each working: a wear meter on the working screen and the station in 3D (cracks in the hearth, a
  sagging bench). Hotter, longer workings wear it faster; Relic and Mythic workings wear it most.
- **Upkeep:** REPAIR for a small sum any time brings the wear down. Past three quarters worn, the band narrows a little and the meter
  glows.
- **Broken:** at full wear the station breaks **between** workings, never during one. A broken forge can't be used until rebuilt:
  **REBUILD** (about a day's earnings, and it's out for a few hours of real time), or **BUILD A NEW ONE**: a better forge
  (the dwarven forge: a wider band, slower wear), the first of a new set of station upgrades.
- **The workshop in 3D** shows the broken forge cold, and the new one going up.

### Thieves
- **The risk:** each night away (and now and then while open), a chance of a break-in that grows with renown and with the worth of
  what's on the shelves, from about one night in forty at SOUGHT to one in twelve at OF THE REACH with nothing to stop it.
- **The warning:** a rumour on the notice board ("A gang out of the marsh road is working the lanes"), a stranger at the counter who
  buys nothing and looks too long at the shelves.
- **What's taken:** one to three pieces from the shelves (the best first) and the coin in the till (a share of the day's takings, not
  your purse). Not the wall, not the Hall, not the bank.
- **The morning after:** the door forced, the shelves bare in 3D, a note from the Watch naming the gang and where they went.
- **The defences:**
  - **A lock and a strongbox** (furniture, 400 c and 1,500 c): fewer break-ins, and the till's coin is safe.
  - **A night guard** (a new staff role, a wage): a guard on duty stops most break-ins outright, and catches some thieves.
  - **Wards** (made, not bought: a warding oil at the kettle, a ward-lantern at the glass): placed in the shop like decor, each lowers
    the risk and adds a little charm.

### The hunt: going after the thieves
- After a robbery, the Watch's note opens **a new runner route: AFTER THE GANG** (6–10 hours, while the trail is warm: two days).
- **The outcome:** the stolen pieces back (most of the time), a bounty from the Watch in coin and renown, and now and then the gang's
  own loot (a found tile, a rare material). Sometimes the trail goes cold and they come back empty.
- **The risk:** the runner can be hurt (see below). Armour you made, given to the runner, cuts the risk.

### The runner in harm's way
- **Gear:** a runner can be given pieces you made: a jerkin, boots, a helm, a weapon. Gear lowers the danger of every trip and wears
  as they travel (a piece can come back worn or broken).
- **Hurt:** on dangerous routes (below the deep doors, after the gang, and any road during a monster attack) a runner can come back
  hurt: off work for a few days, a healer's bill (Marrow's salves help, and you can make them).
- **Killed:** rare, only on the most dangerous trips, and much rarer with good gear. The runner is gone; their name goes on a stone in
  the shop (a memorial piece of decor); a new runner can be hired. **This one is your call: see the questions below.**

### Monsters
- **New town events with a build-up over a few days:** wolves in the lanes, a grave-wight on the ford road, something out of the deep
  doors. First rumours, then sightings, then the night it comes.
- **Arm the Watch:** while one threatens, the Watch posts **a big order on the notice board**: several spears, a few helms, arrows by
  the sheaf. Filling it in time pays well, adds renown, and keeps the attack off the lanes.
- **If it comes:** fewer customers for a day or two, runner routes closed or dangerous, and on a bad night damage to the shop:
  furniture broken (a repair bill), a window out, a shelf knocked over (its pieces safe in the back room). Wards and a guard lower the
  damage.
- **A hunt of its own:** the bigger monsters can be hunted by the Watch with your weapons, or by your runner well armed, for a trophy
  (a rare material, a wall piece).

### A living world (the texture between threats)
- **Seasons:** a turn of the season every two real weeks: winter brings furs and fewer travellers, summer the fair and the road trade.
- **Rumours and news:** a short line or two a day on the notice board, some true, some not: what's coming, who's in town, what sold
  well in the Landing.
- **Regulars' lives:** the regulars come back changed: Vance promoted after an attack you helped stop, Ketty's shop robbed by the same
  gang.
- **Festivals and the market:** market days, a guild fair where the best piece in the Reach wins a prize.

## Questions (answered below)

1. **Can a runner die?** Recommended: yes, but rare (about 1 trip in 150 below the deep doors with no gear, far less with gear, never
   on the short roads), always with a warning before a dangerous trip. The alternative is hurt only.
2. **Can thieves take a Relic from a shelf?** Recommended: yes, and AFTER THE GANG always gets a Relic back if the runner reaches them
   (a story, not a loss). Never from the Hall, never a Mythic (it can't be on a shelf).
3. **Loan interest while away:** recommended: it counts in real weeks, but the bailiff only comes while the page is open. (Decided otherwise: paused while away.)
4. **The gentler Reach:** recommended: offered on every new game and switchable later under the Bank; existing saves start with the
   threats on but a week's grace before the first one.
5. **Order:** recommended as listed (the Bank first, as it's self-contained and gives players a way to absorb the later losses).

## Decided (2026-11-16)

1. **A runner can die, rarely:** only on the most dangerous trips, with a warning first; gear you made cuts the chance a lot. Runners
   can be equipped (milestone 22). A memorial stone goes in the shop.
2. **Thieves can take a Relic from a shelf;** AFTER THE GANG always recovers it if the runner reaches them. Never from the Hall.
3. **Loan interest is paused while away:** only taken for weeks in which the game was opened.
4. **The gentler Reach:** offered on every new game, switchable later under the Bank; existing saves get a week's grace.
5. **Order:** as listed, the Bank first.

## Milestone 19, as built (release 38)

- **THE BANK** is a fifth place in the top bar (js/bank.js, no page access; tests/bank.test.mjs).
- **The account:** PUT IN, TAKE OUT, ALL MY PURSE. Coin there can't be stolen. 1 % a week, at most 400 c.
- **A loan:** from KNOWN (1,000 c; SOUGHT 3,000; FAMED 8,000; RENOWNED 20,000; OF THE REACH 40,000). 5 % a week, from the purse, then
  the account. Short of it: a letter, the unpaid part and a 10 % fee onto the loan. A second miss: the bailiff takes shelf pieces, best
  first, toward twice what was missed (each piece's worth off the loan), never a Relic or a Mythic, and renown drops by 10. A loan never
  grows past twice what was borrowed.
- **The week:** seven real days, counted only when the game is open at its end: a long time away counts as one week (interest paused
  while away, as decided). The bank's book lists every week and every move.
- **The gentler Reach:** a tick-box on every empty slot ("A gentler Reach: no thieves, monsters or dangers"), and SPARE THIS SAVE / LET
  THE REACH IN under the Bank. Saves from before get a week's grace (`threatsFrom`). Nothing reads it yet: the threats come from
  milestone 20. The bank's own bailiff isn't a threat: it comes with a loan the player chose.
- **Sim (45 days, seeds 1–3, `BANK=1`):** the bot borrows for the second shop the day it's allowed and repays before interest falls due;
  income is unchanged. The bank is no shortcut for a steady player; it helps one short of coin.

## Milestone 20, as built (release 39)

- **Wear** (js/wear.js, tests/wear.test.mjs): each working wears its station, out of 100: a plain working ½, MAKE IT ¼, a rework 1,
  a part 1½, a Relic working 3, a Mythic 5 (about 200 plain workings from new to broken). By the kind of working, not yet by how hot
  or long it ran.
- **Worn** from 75 %: the band ×0.92, a toast, a red wear bar, a line on PREPARE.
- **Broken** at 100, after the working (never during one): WORK IT and MAKE IT show BROKEN (or REBUILDING, with the time left), the
  workshop's fire light goes dim in 3D. A rework at a broken station can still be left (LEAVE IT).
- **THE STATIONS** (THE FORGE › WORKSHOP): each station's wear, REPAIR (2 c a point, +50 % a renown rung) and REBUILD (300 c a rung
  above UNKNOWN, from 300; 3 hours).
- **The dwarven forge:** from FAMED, 8,000 c, out 6 hours while it goes up; then the band ×1.08 and wear ×0.6. No new 3D model yet:
  the workshop shows the same forge.
- **Spared** on a gentler Reach and in the week of grace.
- **Sim (45 days, seeds 1–3; `WEAR=0` to compare):** a bot that repairs past 60 % spends about 1 % of its earnings on upkeep and never
  breaks a station. Wear is a small, steady sink; it bites only if neglected.

## Milestone 21, as built (release 40)

- **Thieves** (js/thieves.js, tests/thieves.test.mjs). From SOUGHT, once a day there's a chance a gang starts working the lanes: about
  one day in 40 at SOUGHT, 28 at FAMED, 18 at RENOWNED, 12 OF THE REACH, up to half as likely again with a rich shelf.
- **The rumour first:** a toast, a notice on the town board, and STAFF › THE NIGHT. From the next day, for four days, each night away
  of four hours or more they try the door.
- **The door:** they get in 60 % of the time; a lock makes it 40 % fewer; a night guard turns three quarters away (a Stout one more)
  and catches about a third (a Watchful one more): caught, the gang is gone and the town is grateful (+10 renown).
- **In:** one to three of the shelf's pieces, the best first (a Relic too: kept in `save.robbery` for the hunt, milestone 22), and
  30 % of that night's takings from the till (none with a strongbox). Never the wall, the Hall, the bank, or the second shop.
- **The morning after:** the away report says who, what and which way they went; the shelves are bare where the pieces were. No
  forced-door scene in 3D yet.
- **STAFF › THE NIGHT:** the risk tonight, the rumour, the last robbery and its trail, A GOOD LOCK (400 c), A STRONGBOX (1,500 c). The
  **night guard** is a third staff role (LOOKING FOR WORK, from SOUGHT; wage 5 c an hour).
- Thieves come only on a night away (the game closed or hidden four hours or more), not while it's open.
- **Sim (60 days, seeds 1–4):** with a lock and strongbox but no guard (`GUARD=0`), 1–4 robberies, losing 0.1–0.5 % of earnings; with
  a night guard from FAMED, 0–1 robberies and the rest caught or turned away. `THIEVES=0` turns them off.

## Milestone 22, as built (release 41)

- **The runner's gear** (js/runner.js, tests/runner.test.mjs): eight slots on the runner's card (STAFF › ON THE BOOKS): WEAPON
  (falchion, dagger, axe, staff, shortbow, longbow), HEAD (helm, hood), BODY (jerkin), HANDS (gloves), FEET (boots), RING, and two
  POTIONS. Tap a slot: the pieces in the back room that fit, best first; GIVE IT, or TAKE IT BACK. Carried pieces are off the shelves.
  A potion is one bottle from a batch you brewed (taken back, it's one bottle again).
- **Power by tier:** Common 1, Fine 2, Rare 3, Old-Blood 5, Relic 8, Mythic 12 (a flawed piece one less). Armour and half the ring
  guard; the weapon fights (an oil makes it half as strong again). The card shows guard, fight, and the odds below the deep doors and
  on the hunt.
- **Danger** on the hard roads: hurt and killed, both less with gear (hurt × 6 / (6 + guard + fight / 2), killed × that squared).
  Bare, below the deep doors: hurt 12 %, killed 0.7 %; after the gang 18 % and 1 %; into the deep doors and out to the Reach a little
  hurt, never killed; the short roads none. A dangerous trip asks first, with the odds.
- **Potions,** used up: a salve heals a wound on the road, a draught keeps a dying runner alive (hurt instead), a tonic helps the hunt
  (+15 %), an oil sharpens the weapon; a tonic and an oil are used on any hard road.
- **Hurt:** resting 2–4 days (can't be sent), a healer's bill, and one piece of armour broken (never a Relic or Mythic).
- **Killed:** gone from the staff; the Watch brings home what they carried (not the potions); their name is REMEMBERED under ON THE
  BOOKS. No memorial stone in 3D yet.
- **A good weapon helps every save:** fewer trips below the deep doors come back empty.
- **The hunt:** AFTER THE GANG appears on the runner's roads while a robbery's trail is warm (2 days): 8 hours, 60 c, dangerous. The
  odds (45 %, more with fight, level and a tonic, at most 95 %) are set as they leave. Caught: the stolen pieces back and a bounty (120 c
  a renown rung) and +8 renown. Cold: gone, unless a Relic was taken: then the Watch keeps the trail warm and the hunt can go again.
- Letting a runner go brings their gear back.
- **Sim (90 days, seeds 1–6, `KIT=0` for none):** a bot that kits its runner from the back room reaches guard 4.5–7.5, fight 2–3; it
  runs below the deep doors nonstop and loses 0–2 runners in 90 days, hunts 0–3 times.
- **Every road has a risk level** (added before release 41 shipped): LOW, MODERATE, HIGH or DEADLY, from the road's danger as the
  runner is kitted now (hurt + 8 × killed: under 1 %, 4 %, 10 %, above). Each road's button shows the level, the odds, and the level it
  would be bare, struck through, when gear brings it down. The short roads are a little risky too (down to the yard: hurt 0.2 %; the
  ford: 0.8 %; never killed); into the deep doors can now kill (0.2 % bare). Bare: yard and ford LOW, the Reach MODERATE, into the deep
  doors HIGH, below the deep doors and after the gang DEADLY. A full set of Rare gear brings below the deep doors down to MODERATE. The
  runner's card says how much the gear takes off every road's risk. A HIGH or DEADLY road asks first.

## Milestone 23, as built (release 42)

- **Monsters** (js/monsters.js, tests/monsters.test.mjs): about one day in 30, something threatens the town: **wolves in the lanes**
  (from KNOWN), **a grave-wight on the ford road** (from SOUGHT), **something out of the deep doors** (from RENOWNED). One at a time.
- **The build-up:** a toast and a notice on the board (THE SHOP › IN TOWN): the rumour the first day, then sightings, then three days
  on, the night it comes. The notice counts down.
- **Arm the Watch:** the notice carries the Watch's order (wolves: 2 blades and 2 sheaves of arrows; the wight: 2 silver weapons and a
  lens or pane for a lantern; the deep thing: 3 Fine weapons, 2 Fine helms or hoods, a Fine bow). GIVE TO THE WATCH from the back room;
  each piece paid half again its worth. Filled in time: kept off the lanes, renown (+15, +25, +40).
- **The night it comes** (the order short): a repair bill (300, 900 or 3,000 c × (1 + rung) / 2), up to three pieces knocked off the
  shelves (safe in the back room), customers scared off for a day or two (they come 1.6× less often). Reported while open or on return.
- **While it's about** (the rumour until a day after it came), every road is twice as dangerous: the risk levels show it.
- **Wards** (STAFF › THE NIGHT, three slots): an oil from the kettle (one bottle), a lens or a pane from the glass. They take off the
  damage (power / (power + 6): a Fine pane and oil, 40 %) and keep some thieves away (half that off their chance at the door). No ward
  in 3D yet, and no monster hunt or trophy (later).
- **Spared** on a gentler Reach.
- **Sim (90 days, seeds 1–4; `MONSTERS=0` to turn off):** 1–3 monsters; the bot (arming the Watch only from what's already in the back
  room) keeps off about one in three; the nights it came cost 0.03–0.4 % of earnings.

## Milestone 24, as built (release 43)

- **Seasons** (js/town.js, tests/living.test.mjs): spring, summer, autumn, winter, each 14 days, the same for everyone (by the date).
  Each season's wares fetch an eighth more (winter: jerkins, hoods, gloves, boots, salves, tonics; summer: bows, arrows, rings, goblets,
  draughts; and so on); summer brings more travellers (customers 10 % more often), winter fewer (15 % less). A notice on the board says
  which season, how far in, and what's wanted; the board's date carries it too.
- **Market days** every seventh day and **the guild fair** every 28th: more customers.
- **The guild fair** (js/living.js): on its day, from NOTED, ENTER THE GUILD FAIR on the board: one piece from anywhere (it stays
  yours), judged on its worth against three of the Reach's makers (their pieces scaled to your renown). First, second and third win
  150, 70 or 30 c a renown rung above UNKNOWN and +20, +10, +5 renown. Once a fair. The board says how many days to the next.
- **The word in town:** two or three lines a day on the board, the same all day: true things first (the season turning or about to,
  the fair coming, a gang counting doors, the Watch made captain after a monster kept off, the carpenters busy after one came, the
  Watch drinking to your runner after a hunt, Ketty's stall turned over by the gang that robbed you, your best seller, the Landing),
  then a line of gossip.
- **The regulars' lives** show only through the word in town for now; their stories themselves don't change yet.
- **Sim (90 days, seeds 1–3):** seasons and market days run in every day; the bot entered 2 fairs, placing first or second in half.
- Not seen in the browser: the fair entry (no fair on the day it was checked).
