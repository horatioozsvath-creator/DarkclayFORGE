# The living Reach: a plan (milestones 19–24)

Status: planned, nothing built. Builds on release 37.

## What it's for

Right now the Reach is kind: nothing happens to the shop that the player didn't choose. The late game also piles up coin with little to
spend it on (BALANCE-NOTES: 5,000–13,000 c a day by week four). This plan adds a world that acts on its own: thieves, monsters, a forge
that wears out, a bank, and a runner who can come to harm. Each threat has a warning, a defence you can buy or make, and a way back.

## Rules for every threat

1. **Seen coming.** Every threat shows first: rumours on the notice board, a sign on the door, the forge's wear. Nothing lands without
   a warning the player could act on.
2. **A defence for coin, and a better one you make.** Guards and locks can be bought; the best wards, armour and weapons for the Watch
   are made at your own stations. The crafting stays the heart of the game.
3. **Never ruinous.** A bad night costs a share of what's on show, never the save. Coin in the bank is always safe. Relics and Mythics
   in the Hall are never taken. Every loss can be won back or rebuilt.
4. **Scaled to the shop.** Threats grow with renown and what's on the shelves: a new shop isn't worth robbing, a famed one is.
5. **A gentler Reach.** Each save can turn the threats off (a switch on the title screen's new game, and under the Bank).
6. **Told plainly.** What happened while you were away is the first thing you see on return, with what it cost and what to do next.

## The systems

### The Bank (a new tab beside STAFF)
- **Deposits:** coin in the bank can't be stolen. A small interest each week (1 %), capped so it's never a money machine.
- **Loans:** from KNOWN, borrow up to a limit set by renown (KNOWN 1,000 c up to OF THE REACH 40,000 c). **Interest is 5 % a week**,
  taken every seven days from your coin (or the bank, if you choose). Repay any time, all or part.
- **Missing a payment:** the first time, a letter and a late fee. The second, **the bailiff** comes in person and takes pieces from the
  shelf to the value owed (never Relics or Mythics; the Hall is never touched), and renown drops a little. The debt never grows past
  twice what was borrowed.
- **While away for a long time:** interest is counted per week of real time, like the shop's clock. The bailiff only comes while the
  page is open, so a long holiday ends with a letter, not empty shelves.
- **What it's for:** buying the second shop, the Hall or a station early, then paying it back from what it earns.

### Wear, and the forge that breaks
- **Every station wears** with each working: a wear meter on the working screen and the station in 3D (cracks in the hearth, a
  sagging bench). Hotter, longer workings wear it faster; Relic and Mythic workings wear it most.
- **Upkeep:** REPAIR for a small sum any time brings the wear down. Past three quarters worn, the band narrows a little and the meter
  glows.
- **Broken:** at full wear the station breaks **between** workings, never during one. A broken forge can't be used until rebuilt:
  **REBUILD** (about a day's earnings, and it's out for a few hours of real time), or **BUILD A NEW ONE**: a better forge
  (the dwarven forge: a wider band, slower wear), the first of a new set of station upgrades.
- **The workshop in 3D** shows the broken forge cold, and the new one going up.

### Thieves
- **The risk:** each night away (and now and then while open), a chance of a break-in that grows with renown and with the worth of
  what's on the shelves, from about one night in forty at SOUGHT to one in twelve at OF THE REACH with nothing to stop it.
- **The warning:** a rumour on the notice board ("A gang out of the marsh road is working the lanes"), a stranger at the counter who
  buys nothing and looks too long at the shelves.
- **What's taken:** one to three pieces from the shelves (the best first) and the coin in the till (a share of the day's takings, not
  your purse). Not the wall, not the Hall, not the bank.
- **The morning after:** the door forced, the shelves bare in 3D, a note from the Watch naming the gang and where they went.
- **The defences:**
  - **A lock and a strongbox** (furniture, 400 c and 1,500 c): fewer break-ins, and the till's coin is safe.
  - **A night guard** (a new staff role, a wage): a guard on duty stops most break-ins outright, and catches some thieves.
  - **Wards** (made, not bought: a warding oil at the kettle, a ward-lantern at the glass): placed in the shop like decor, each lowers
    the risk and adds a little charm.

### The hunt: going after the thieves
- After a robbery, the Watch's note opens **a new runner route: AFTER THE GANG** (6–10 hours, while the trail is warm: two days).
- **The outcome:** the stolen pieces back (most of the time), a bounty from the Watch in coin and renown, and now and then the gang's
  own loot (a found tile, a rare material). Sometimes the trail goes cold and they come back empty.
- **The risk:** the runner can be hurt (see below). Armour you made, given to the runner, cuts the risk.

### The runner in harm's way
- **Gear:** a runner can be given pieces you made: a jerkin, boots, a helm, a weapon. Gear lowers the danger of every trip and wears
  as they travel (a piece can come back worn or broken).
- **Hurt:** on dangerous routes (below the deep doors, after the gang, and any road during a monster attack) a runner can come back
  hurt: off work for a few days, a healer's bill (Marrow's salves help, and you can make them).
- **Killed:** rare, only on the most dangerous trips, and much rarer with good gear. The runner is gone; their name goes on a stone in
  the shop (a memorial piece of decor); a new runner can be hired. **This one is your call: see the questions below.**

### Monsters
- **New town events with a build-up over a few days:** wolves in the lanes, a grave-wight on the ford road, something out of the deep
  doors. First rumours, then sightings, then the night it comes.
- **Arm the Watch:** while one threatens, the Watch posts **a big order on the notice board**: several spears, a few helms, arrows by
  the sheaf. Filling it in time pays well, adds renown, and keeps the attack off the lanes.
- **If it comes:** fewer customers for a day or two, runner routes closed or dangerous, and on a bad night damage to the shop:
  furniture broken (a repair bill), a window out, a shelf knocked over (its pieces safe in the back room). Wards and a guard lower the
  damage.
- **A hunt of its own:** the bigger monsters can be hunted by the Watch with your weapons, or by your runner well armed, for a trophy
  (a rare material, a wall piece).

### A living world (the texture between threats)
- **Seasons:** a turn of the season every two real weeks: winter brings furs and fewer travellers, summer the fair and the road trade.
- **Rumours and news:** a short line or two a day on the notice board, some true, some not: what's coming, who's in town, what sold
  well in the Landing.
- **Regulars' lives:** the regulars come back changed: Vance promoted after an attack you helped stop, Ketty's shop robbed by the same
  gang.
- **Festivals and the market:** market days, a guild fair where the best piece in the Reach wins a prize.

## Questions to decide before building

1. **Can a runner die?** Recommended: yes, but rare (about 1 trip in 150 below the deep doors with no gear, far less with gear, never
   on the short roads), always with a warning before a dangerous trip. The alternative is hurt only.
2. **Can thieves take a Relic from a shelf?** Recommended: yes, and AFTER THE GANG always gets a Relic back if the runner reaches them
   (a story, not a loss). Never from the Hall, never a Mythic (it can't be on a shelf).
3. **Loan interest while away:** recommended as above: it counts in real weeks, but the bailiff only comes while the page is open.
4. **The gentler Reach:** recommended: offered on every new game and switchable later under the Bank; existing saves start with the
   threats on but a week's grace before the first one.
5. **Order:** recommended as listed (the Bank first, as it's self-contained and gives players a way to absorb the later losses).
