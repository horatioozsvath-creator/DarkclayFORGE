# Milestone 57: the Nine of the Hearth, and the climb (release 84)

Asked for: the fourth part of the third region (docs/NORTH-PLAN.md). **The High Snow is open.** From this release, a Maker of the
Nine who hands on the forge chooses from three places: the Reach, over the pass on the Greyshore, or up into the snow in
Hearthold. Milestones 54–56 built the hold unseen; this one lets players reach it.

## The Nine of the Hearth
Aud the Hearthkeeper kept the fire alight through the Long Winter, when the ice took the lower halls. Nine pieces were sworn to
her hearth, and they went down into the ice with the halls. Each is one of milestone 56's Relics, reforged with its shard and three
parts from three other stations, as the other Nines are.

| Mythic | From | Its shard |
|---|---|---|
| **The Axe of the High Gate** | the Frostwarden | an order from the Thanes' Moot |
| **Aud's Ring** | the Thane's Ring | the ice-foot, when the thaw gives back |
| **The Oath-Axe** | the Moot Axe | below the old halls |
| **The Hood of the Long Dark** | the Gallery Hood | below the old halls |
| **The Glacier-Walkers** | the Fellstride | the ice-foot |
| **The Bow of the Last Pass** | the Pass-Bow | an order from the Thanes' Moot |
| **The Hearth-Draught** | the Keepers' Ember | the ice-foot |
| **Aud's Eyes** | the Snow-Seer's Glass | below the old halls |
| **The Hearth-Fire** | the Hearthkeeper's Lamp | an order from the Thanes' Moot |

- **Where the shards are,** three from each place, as in the other regions:
  - **below the old halls,** on a runner's trip (as below the deep doors);
  - **the Thanes' Moot's orders** (as the Court's): a Rare or better piece of the Relic's kind, and a shard with the pay. The Moot
    also asks, once, to buy a Mythic;
  - **the ice-foot,** on the day **the thaw gives back**: the hold's own town event, in place of the fallen star. GO UP TO THE
    ICE-FOOT, and up to two shards lie in the meltwater. Now and then a runner finds one below the old halls instead.
- A generation on, the shards are scattered again, to the same count of each place, among the Hearth's Nine.
- **The ending.** All Nine of the Hearth made: the maker's mark is **MAKER OF THE HEARTH**, with the ending in the hold's words.
  The Hall's charm doubles, the title is on the forge's sign, the flyer and the link, and the forge can be handed on.
- **Each region's Nine count apart.** Up in the snow only the Hearth's make the ending. Until this release the Reach's Nine stood
  in up there (milestone 54); now the hold has its own.

## The climb
- **At the handing on**, the dialog asks WHERE: **THE REACH** (the Nine of Veric), **OVER THE PASS** (Saltby, and the Nine of the
  Tide) or **UP INTO THE SNOW** (Hearthold, and the Nine of the Hearth). It's offered at every handing on, from the first.
- **A new game always starts in the Reach.**
- **The heir keeps everything the line keeps,** whichever way they go: the book, the blueprints, the found tiles, the trades,
  the trait, the mine's depth and the heirlooms.
- **Hearthold's opening.** The first heir up into the snow gets their own first morning: four days up the mule track, the gate,
  and a hold round a hearth that has never gone out, where nobody has heard of the line or of the Nines below.
- **Each region's words for the road.** The dialog and its confirm say "up in the snow in Hearthold" or "over the pass in
  Saltby", and an heir going home says "back down from the snow" or "back over the pass". A later heir who comes back to a region
  hears that it was a generation ago "in the Reach", "over the pass" or "up in the snow", wherever it was.
- **The town's welcome** (the shop starts KNOWN from the third generation) counts, up in the snow, only the generations that
  worked there, as on the coast.

## The line's record, the link and the flyer
- The record reads "SECOND OF THE LINE · NAME · OF THE HIGH SNOW", and a maker up there who made the Nine reads "Maker of the
  Hearth".
- The visit link carries the region (`rg: 'north'`); the visitor sees "· OF THE HIGH SNOW", the hold's top rung (OF THE SNOW)
  and MAKER OF THE HEARTH. An older link, or a region it can't read, is the Reach, as before.
- The flyer reads "forge & shop · second of the line · of the High Snow".
- **The rest of the game is told of the hold now:** a material sold only up there says "Sold in the High Snow, up in the snow",
  Aud's Delving is named among the mines, and heartwood's line names the Snow Carrier.

- **The Reach and the coast are unchanged:** their runs (the Reach 120 days with luck on and off, the coast 90 days) are
  byte-identical to release 80.
