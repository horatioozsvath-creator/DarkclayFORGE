# Rarity to Mythic: the full ladder for every station

Status: milestones 11–18 built (releases 22–36). The pace target (all Nine in about three months) is not met yet: see milestone 18. The Nine can be made. Builds on release 21. Darkclay Zero's ladder has six tiers (`data/zero/tiers.json`); the Forge has built four.
This plan takes every station up the last two: **Relic** (named, one of a kind) and **Mythic** (the Nine).

## The ladder

| Tier | Frame | Stats | Affixes | Worth | How it's made | Who buys it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | bone | ×1.00 | 0 | ×1 | rolled | anyone |
| Fine | green | ×1.10 | 1 | ×2 | rolled | most |
| Rare | blue | ×1.22 | 2 | ×4 | rolled, or reworked up | guards, delvers, factors |
| Old-Blood | violet, foil | ×1.36 | 3 | ×8 | a master's last rework; at the kettle, a master's brew | stewards, collectors |
| **Relic** | **amber, foil** | ×1.52 *(Zero)* | 3 rolled **and its own power** | **×20** | **a named pattern, worked on an Old-Blood base with a relic material** | **the Crown's agent, relic orders** |
| **Mythic** | **red, foil, glow** | ×1.70 *(Zero)* | **its own three powers** | **×50** | **a Relic reforged with a shard and parts from three stations** | **the Court, once; or kept** |

Relics and Mythics are **unique**: each named piece exists once in a save. Making one is the game's late goal; selling one is a
choice, not the point.

## Relics, station by station

Each Relic is **a named pattern** (a blueprint card you earn), worked on **an Old-Blood piece of its kind** with **a relic
material**, at **trade 9**. The Relic working asks for **WHITE HEAT twice and not one crack**. Fall short and the piece stays
Old-Blood, untouched; the material is spent. Each has a **power** of its own on top of three affixes.

| Station | Relic | Its base | Its material | Its power (examples) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forge | **Gatewarden** | falchion | heartstone | holds the line: parries break a charge |
| Forge | **The Widow's Band** | ring | moonsilver | a vow kept: the wearer can't be turned from a path |
| Forge | **The Hollow King's Helm** | helm | heartstone | no fear: the wearer stands when others run |
| Stretcher | **Wolfmantle** | jerkin | wyrm-hide | the pack's warmth: cold and wet don't touch it |
| Stretcher | **The Roadwalkers** | boots | wyrm-hide | the long road: a day's march costs half |
| Bench | **The Oskbow** | longbow | ghost-yew | the river's reach: half again the range |
| Bench | **The Abbey Staff** | staff | starglass core | it remembers: a spell cast once comes easier |
| Kettle | **Wenna's Last Draught** | tonic (a batch of one) | moon-mead | a night's sleep in a swallow |
| Kettle | **Brannoc's Salve** | salve (a batch of one) | moon-mead | closes any wound by morning |
| Glass | **The Abbey Lens** | lens | starglass core | sees through a lie written down |
| Glass | **The Corrow Goblet** | goblet | moonsilver | poison shows in it |

Eleven Relics: three at the forge, two at each other station. **Bottles from the glass never go past Rare**: they're stock.

**Where blueprints come from:**
- **The regulars' great stories:** after their first story, each regular comes back at RENOWNED with a longer one (five steps), ending
  in a blueprint. Ketty → The Widow's Band; Vance → Gatewarden; Bram → The Roadwalkers; Corrow → The Corrow Goblet; Marrow → Brannoc's
  Salve.
- **The Deep:** a runner's long trip can bring one back.
- **Town events:** a fair's travelling smith sells one, once.

**Who buys a Relic:** the Crown's agent (from RENOWNED; purse 8,000–25,000 c), and relic orders from the regulars. A Relic on the
wall, or in the Hall, is worth 80 renown and draws master collectors.

## Mythic: the Nine

Darkclay Zero's Mythics are the Nine of Veric. Here each is **a Relic reforged**, and it needs the whole workshop:

1. **The Relic** it grows from.
2. **A shard of Veric**, one for each of the Nine, found on **the trail of the Nine** (from OF THE REACH). The trail runs through the
   Deep, the Court's orders and the rarest town events, one clue at a time.
3. **Three parts from three stations**, each its own special working: for example, a starglass edge from the glass, a moonwell quench
   from the kettle, a wyrm-hide grip from the stretcher.
4. **The Mythic working** at the Relic's own station: three heats, the narrowest band in the game, and not one crack.

| # | Mythic | From the Relic | Its parts (three stations) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Veric's Edge** | Gatewarden (forge) | glass: starglass edge · kettle: moonwell quench · stretcher: wyrm-hide grip |
| 2 | **The Iron Crown** | The Hollow King's Helm (forge) | glass: a crown of panes · stretcher: a lining · bench: a bentwood band |
| 3 | **The Ring of the Nine** | The Widow's Band (forge) | glass: a stone of starglass · kettle: a vow-oil · bench: a ring-box |
| 4 | **The Mantle of the Reach** | Wolfmantle (stretcher) | forge: clasps · kettle: a proofing · glass: a mirror-brooch |
| 5 | **The Long Song** | The Oskbow (bench) | forge: starmetal nocks · stretcher: a wyrm-sinew string · glass: a sighting lens |
| 6 | **The Staff of Osk** | The Abbey Staff (bench) | forge: a ferrule · glass: a heart of starglass · kettle: a binding oil |
| 7 | **Veric's Draught** | Wenna's Last Draught (kettle) | glass: a Mythic flask · forge: a silver stopper · bench: a yew cup |
| 8 | **The Lens of Far Seeing** | The Abbey Lens (glass) | forge: a frame · bench: a staff mount · kettle: a clearing oil |
| 9 | **The Window of Morning** | The Corrow Goblet (glass) | forge: a leading · stretcher: a hanging · kettle: a staining |

Every station makes one or two of the Nine and a part for others, so **the Nine need every station set up**.

**A Mythic isn't sold to ordinary buyers.** The Court orders each one once (pays ×2 its worth, about 30,000–120,000 c), or it's kept in
the Hall. **Making all Nine** is the end of the game: the maker's mark becomes *Maker of the Nine*, the Hall's charm doubles, and
every shop link carries the Nine.

## Around them

- **The Deep:** a new road for the runner, from RENOWNED. Long trips (8–12 hours) that can come back empty, bringing relic
  materials, the odd blueprint, and, on the trail, shards.
- **The Hall:** a third room (25,000 c, from RENOWNED), a gallery of plinths and cases for Relics and Mythics, in 3D. Each piece in it
  adds charm and renown. It shows in shop links and flyers.
- **Relic and Mythic cards:** the amber and red frames, a foil sheen and a glow, the power written under the name, a lore of its own.
- **Coin sinks** for the late game: relic materials (2,000–6,000 c), the Hall, the Deep's trips, the Mythic parts' workings.

## Decided (2026-11-16)

1. **Unique pieces:** one of each Relic and Mythic in a save.
2. **Powers:** words on the card, as affixes are.
3. **The Nine's names and lore:** the names above.
4. **Pace:** the first Relic in about four weeks of steady play, all Nine in about three months.

## Milestone 11, as built (release 22)

- **RENOWNED** (1,200; 1,000 from release 23) and **OF THE REACH** (3,500) above FAMED. Purses stop growing at FAMED, and stewards and master collectors
  level off there: the rungs above bring new things instead of fuller purses.
- **The Crown's agent** comes to a RENOWNED shop (more often OF THE REACH): purse 8,000–25,000 c, Old-Blood or better only.
- **Below the deep doors**, a runner's road from RENOWNED: 12 hours, 400 c, three rare things, a found tile or an old piece maybe,
  and **relic materials** (one about 60 % of the time, a second 15 %), but about one trip in seven comes back with little.
- **Six relic materials**, found only there: heartstone, moonsilver, wyrm-hide, ghost-yew, starglass core, moon-mead. They keep in the
  store room until milestone 12 gives them a use.
- **Sim, 60 days, seeds 1–4:** FAMED on day 9–10, **RENOWNED on day 29–32**, renown about 2,500 by day 60 (OF THE REACH not yet:
  Relics on the wall and in the Hall will add to it). The Crown's agent bought 1–3 pieces over 1,000 c by day 60.


## Milestone 12, as built (release 23)

- **The Relic tier** (amber, foil, ×1.52, worth ×20, three affixes and a power). A plain rework now stops at Old-Blood: only the Relic
  working goes past it.
- **The forge's three Relics:** Gatewarden (falchion, heartstone), The Widow's Band (ring, moonsilver), The Hollow King's Helm (helm,
  heartstone). One of each in a save, ever.
- **Blueprints:** Captain Vance's and Ketty's great stories (five steps each, from RENOWNED, after their first story); and the Deep, on
  12 % of trips (any blueprint still to be found, one for a trade at 7 or more first). Listed under THE FORGE › WORKSHOP.
- **The Relic working:** from the back room, on an Old-Blood piece of its kind, at trade 9, with the blueprint: three times the bars and
  one relic material. The band ×0.7 of the usual, WHITE HEAT after 12 clean, and it must come **twice** with **not one crack**. Short of
  that, the piece stays Old-Blood as it was.
- **The named card:** its name, its lore, its power under the name; shared links carry the power.
- **Relic orders:** a RENOWNED shop holding a blueprint is sometimes asked for that Relic (12 % of orders), paying twice its worth.
- **Tuning, from the sim (75 days, seeds 1–6, now making to order and sending a runner below):** at 1,200 and 8 %, the first Relic came
  on day 39–63 (one seed none). **RENOWNED down to 1,000 and the Deep's blueprints up to 12 %**: RENOWNED on day 17–21, **the first Relic
  on day 27–63 (median about day 42)**, OF THE REACH on day 63–68. Slower than the four weeks planned for some runs; the next milestones'
  Relics (more patterns to work) should bring the median down.

## Milestone 15, as built (release 26)

## Milestone 16, as built (release 28)

- **The Mythic tier** (red, foil, ×1.70, worth ×50), and **the Nine** as data: each from its Relic, its three powers, its lore, and its
  three parts from three other stations (milestone 17 makes them).
- **The trail of the Nine** opens OF THE REACH: under THE FORGE › WORKSHOP, each Mythic's clue opens once its Relic is made, and says
  where its **shard of Veric** lies:
  - **below the deep doors** (three of the Nine): a runner's trip finds one 10 % of the time;
  - **the Court** (three): an order for an Old-Blood piece of the Relic's kind, about 15 % of orders while one is waiting, paying twice
    the worth and the shard;
  - **the fallen star** (three): a new town event, about one day in three weeks; GO OUT TO THE FALLEN STAR on the notice board, once.
- Mythic cards show their three powers, and links carry them.
- **Sim (120 days, seeds 1–4):** OF THE REACH on day 59–66; Relics 1–3 by day 120 (the first on day 44–96); **one shard by day 69–100**.
  **All Nine in three months is far off:** each Relic needs trade 9 in its own pattern, and the bot masters only the falchion. This is
  milestone 18's work: likely a lower trade for later Relics (or trade 9 in the station rather than the pattern), and more blueprints
  and shards once the trail is open.

## Milestone 17, as built (release 30)

- **The parts:** each of the Nine's three parts is a special working at its station (worked as that station's dagger, gloves, staff,
  oil or lens), open once its shard is found, the station is set up, and the maker is trade 6 in something there. It costs that piece's
  recipe and two of the station's rare things; the band ×0.8, WHITE HEAT once and not one crack. Short of it, the stuff is spent and it
  can be tried again. Started from THE TRAIL OF THE NINE (a button per part).
- **The Mythic working:** on the Relic, from the back room, with the shard, all three parts and trade 9 in its pattern: three times the
  bars and the Relic's material; the band ×0.6 (the narrowest), WHITE HEAT **three times**, not one crack. Short of it, the Relic stays
  as it was and the shard and parts are kept. The Mythic takes its name, lore and three powers; one in a save; never broken down.
- **Not yet:** the Court's once-only purchase of each Mythic and the ending (milestone 18). The pace finding from milestone 16 stands:
  in the sim almost nobody reaches a Mythic; milestone 18 tunes it.

## Milestone 18, as built (release 36)

- **Mastery by the station:** a Relic or Mythic working now asks **trade 9 in something at the piece's station** (its best pattern
  there) and **trade 5 in the piece's own pattern** (its WHITE HEAT open), not trade 9 in that pattern. Old-Blood still asks trade 6
  in the pattern, so in practice a Relic needs trade 6 there. The Deep's blueprints favour a station the maker is good at.
- **More of the trail:** a trip below finds a shard 35 % of the time (was 10 %), the Court's shard orders are 40 % of orders while one
  waits (was 15 %), the fallen star about one day in ten (was one in three weeks), the Deep's blueprints 22 % (was 12 %).
- **The Court buys each Mythic once:** while the maker holds one of the Nine the Court hasn't asked for, half the special orders are the
  Court's, for that piece, paying twice its worth (+50 renown). Sell it (out of the Hall if it's there) or KEEP IT: either way the Court
  never asks for that one again. A Mythic can't go on a shelf and fills no other order; one shelved before release 36 comes down.
- **The ending, Maker of the Nine:** the ninth Mythic made (sold or kept, made counts) shows the ending; the maker's title becomes
  MAKER OF THE NINE on the forge and shop signs, the save slot, the flyer and shop links (`n9`); the Hall's charm doubles. THE TRAIL OF
  THE NINE keeps a READ THE ENDING AGAIN button.
- **The sim** now works the parts and Mythic workings, and plays like someone chasing the trail (`CHASE=0` for the old bot): from
  RENOWNED it practises the patterns it holds blueprints for, keeps their Old-Blood pieces back, and takes a piece down from the shelf or
  the Hall for a working. **Its numbers aren't comparable with earlier milestones'.**
- **Sim (150 days, seeds 1–6):** the first Relic on day 30–71 (median about 40); 5–8 Relics by day 150; the first Mythic on day 52–91;
  **3–5 of the Nine by day 150**; at 180 days (seeds 1, 3, 5) 4–7. **All Nine in about three months is not met.** Raising the odds
  further did little: shards keep pace with Relics; the tail is each Relic's chain (trade 6 in its own pattern, a Rare, the Old-Blood
  rework, then the Relic working), and the sim's success rates for the workings are fixed guesses. Faster would need a structural change,
  for example every clue open OF THE REACH (shards and parts gathered alongside the Relics), or a Relic piece that needn't be Old-Blood.

## Release 37: every clue at OF THE REACH

- **Every clue of the Nine opens with the trail** (OF THE REACH), not after its Relic, so shards and parts are gathered alongside the
  Relics. The Mythic working still waits on its Relic. The trail lists each Mythic's Relic and whether it's made.
- **Sim (150 days, seeds 1–6):** all 9 shards and all 27 parts by day 150 in every run; the first Mythic on day 52–101; **3–6 of the
  Nine by day 150** (was 3–5). The Relics are the bottleneck now (5–8 of 11 by day 150): each needs trade 6 in its own pattern and an
  Old-Blood piece. Letting a Relic be worked from a Rare would be the next lever.
