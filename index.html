HOW IT WORKS
1 · PREPAREYour strip of clay tiles is laid out face up: what's in it and what put it there. Choose the parts and the found tiles to bring, then WORK IT.
2 · HEAT AND STRIKEHeat it into the gold band. Every strike turns over the next tile: GOOD and GREAT shape it, HOLD does nothing, a CRACK marks it, a found tile does its trick.
3 · MIND THE HEATIt cools as you wait and with every strike. Struck cold, a CRACK slips into your strip. Back in the fire, every tile returns and is shuffled.
4 · PUSH, THEN QUENCHOnce SHAPE is full it's made; every strike pushes the odds of Fine and Rare. Quench when you choose. Practice raises your trade, and a clean, fine working turns up a new found tile.
